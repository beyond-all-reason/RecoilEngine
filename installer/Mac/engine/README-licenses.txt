Recoil engine for macOS

This archive contains the Recoil engine (GPL v2 or later, see LICENSE and
gpl-2.0.txt), its pr-downloader and the libraries listed in this folder, each
under its own license (Mesa, LLVM, SDL, OpenAL Soft, ...).

Source code of the engine: https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/RecoilEngine

Games, lobbies and their artwork are NOT part of this archive. The launcher
downloads them and they are subject to their own licenses.
