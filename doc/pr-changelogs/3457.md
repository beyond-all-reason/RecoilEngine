 * shore waves: the coastmap is updated on the GPU after terrain changes, which removes the `Update::WorldDrawer::{Sky,Water}` spikes in fights near water. Updated areas are no longer shifted by one texel, areas without a coastline no longer wipe the waves of a nearby coast, and skipped frames no longer leak a texture.
 * fix the terrain keeping its old shape where it changed outside the spectated team's LOS, after a spectator switches to full view or to another team: the heightmap, shading and normals textures were not refreshed. `UnsyncedHeightMapUpdate` for such a switch now arrives on the next draw frame, split into strips.
