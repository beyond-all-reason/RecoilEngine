 * Added an opt-in idle hover for agile aircraft (`agileFlight`) that hold on a point: unit tags `agileHoverBob` and `agileHoverSway` (elmos, default 0 = off) make the aircraft bob up and down and sway to its left and right as if it kept its place against gusts, `agileHoverTilt` (default 1) scales the lean that goes with it. All three are also `MoveCtrl.SetAirMoveTypeData` members and reported by `Spring.GetUnitMoveTypeData`. Landing stays exact, and the motion draws nothing from the synced RNG.
