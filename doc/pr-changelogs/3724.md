### Caveats
 * a game loaded from a `.ssf` save now starts paused at the saved frame, announced like any pause (the `GamePaused` callin fires). To resume at once as before, send `pause 0` when the load is done, for example `Spring.SendCommands("pause 0")` from that `GamePaused` call or the first `Update` when `Spring.GetGameState()` reports a saved game.
