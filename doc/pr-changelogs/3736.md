### Features
 * added `Spring.StopSoundByFilename(soundfile [, channel]) -> boolean stopped`. Stops every playing instance of a sound started with `Spring.PlaySoundFile`, on one channel or on all of them.

### Fixes
 * a sound from `sounds.lua` played under a mixed-case name no longer makes a new sound item on every `Spring.PlaySoundFile` call.

### Caveats
 * the `SoundChannel` docs no longer list numbers: they never worked and still play on general.
 * sounds played under a mixed-case name now obey their `maxconcurrent`.
