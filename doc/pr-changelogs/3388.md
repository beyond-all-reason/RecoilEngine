 * Added an opt-in agile flight regime for strafing aircraft (unit tag `agileFlight`, default false): short legs, the final approach, takeoff and landing are flown with hover-like control, so a plain move order ends exactly on its goal and the aircraft lands there; longer legs and all attack runs stay fixed-wing. Tunables `agileSpeed`, `agileTurnRate`, `agileAccRate`, `agileAltitude`, `cruiseDistance` as unit tags and `MoveCtrl.SetAirMoveTypeData` members (zero derives a default); `Spring.GetUnitMoveTypeData` reports them and `flightRegime`.
 * Added the callin `UnitFlightRegimeChanged(unitID, unitDefID, unitTeam, regime)` with `regime` being `"agile"` or `"cruise"`.
