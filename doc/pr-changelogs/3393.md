 * Agile aircraft (`agileFlight`) in cruise flight no longer brake into the agile regime for a final goal they can not stop on from where they are: they fly past, come round at speed and brake on that pass (at most two passes per goal).
 * Added the unit tags `cruiseEntryAngle` (degrees, default 30), `cruiseEntrySpeed` (share of `agileSpeed`, default 0.6) and `cruiseEntryTurnBoost` (`agileTurnRate` multiplier on the way to a cruise leg, default 3), also `MoveCtrl.SetAirMoveTypeData` members and reported by `Spring.GetUnitMoveTypeData`: they set how readily the agile regime hands over to cruise flight. Zero asks for the default.
