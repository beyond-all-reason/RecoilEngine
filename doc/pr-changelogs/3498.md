### Caveats
 * `debug.getregistry`, `debug.getmetatable` and `debug.setmetatable` are no longer available to unsynced Lua (LuaUI, unsynced LuaRules/LuaGaia, LuaIntro, LuaMenu). They could bypass protected engine userdata metatables and cause type confusion. Diagnostic functions such as `debug.traceback` and `debug.getinfo`, and ordinary table metatables, are unaffected.
